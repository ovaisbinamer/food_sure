In short: SVM and Random Forest are given a short description of each photo (color + texture numbers) and sort it into a category. Custom CNN, MobileNetV2, and ResNet50 look at the photo itself and learn what matters on their own — the two pretrained ones (MobileNetV2, ResNet50) had a head start from already knowing 1.4 million other photos, which is why they clearly beat the from-scratch CNN. Every model is choosing 1 of the same 7 answers (pure spice, or one of two adulterants at 5%, 10%, or 15%) — none of them are doing a simple yes/no "is it fake" check.
